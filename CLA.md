# Contributor License Agreement

Pigeon is Apache 2.0 and always will be. This agreement is not about taking
that away.

It exists so that the project can be relicensed or offered commercially later
without tracking down everyone who has ever sent a patch. Without it, every
contributor keeps their own copyright, and any future change — a commercial
edition, a licence change, a donation to a foundation — needs each of them to
agree individually. Projects that skip this discover the problem years in, when
the people involved have changed jobs and email addresses.

You keep the copyright on your work. You are granting a licence, not signing it
away.

```

```

That's it, and it's once — not per pull request. Your signature is recorded in
this repository.
